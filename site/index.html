THE GAME

Manifest is a love letter to Frontier: Elite II and Frontier: First Encounters, and to the space and trading sims that followed them — some long gone, some, like the X series, still flying. It is built in my own style, from the ground up: gravity first, then orbits, then planets, then everything that lives on them.

There are three great factions, empires in all but name, and two powers that answer to none of them: the Syndicate and the Navy. Every action has consequences. Blow up a ship carrying medical supplies to a plague-stricken system, and the deaths that follow might knock that world's technology back a level.

Be careful. The law is watching, and murder is harder to get away with than you'd think, even in deep space. But there is always a place for outlaws.

BEFORE YOU LAUNCH

  • This is an alpha. It is public because it is easier to be told what is broken than to find it alone. If something breaks, say so on the issues page.
  • It runs in your browser. A recent Chrome, Edge or Firefox with WebGL2. A keyboard and mouse are expected; there are no touch controls yet.
  • The same seed is the same universe, on any machine, forever. The seed sits in the address bar, so you can send someone the exact galaxy you are flying in.
  • Saves live in this browser. Clearing your site data clears your career, and the desktop build keeps its own separate saves.
