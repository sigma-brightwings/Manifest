THE AUTOPILOT STILL FLEW THROUGH PLANETS -- just not surface ones. 88e3504
gave a port ON a world a flight plan; a port AROUND one was still aimed at
down a straight line, and when the station is on the far side of its
planet that line goes through the planet. Measured on kawartha, every
orbital port, ship in a low orbit at 0, 90 and 180 degrees from the
station: 0 and 90 docked every time, 180 put five of six into the ground
inside two real seconds. `routeAround` now sits in front of every
straight-line leg -- the approach, the cruise leg, follow -- and if the
line cuts a world's keep-out (surface plus clearance, or the top of the
air if that is higher) it climbs out, then walks round in secants aimed at
the DESTINATION's height, two tangents per leg. 24 of 24 approaches dock,
including from the pad, and none of them goes under the clearance.

Three more found on the way:
- Gravity was clipped with the steering. Cancelling the pull by adding it
  to the command and clipping the SUM scaled the hold-up down exactly when
  the engine was working hardest; on Mirven I (14 m/s2 against a Talon's
  19) the ship sank hundreds of km every turn. `holdUpThenSteer` pays the
  pull in full and steers with what is left.
- Cruise dropped out INSIDE the air. The mass-lock drop-out fired about
  110 km above bare rock, under the atmosphere top of Mirven I (170) and
  IV (116), and dropCruise handed back a circular orbit in the upper air.
  Clearance is measured to the keep-out now: drop-out at 440-580 km.
- The node autopilot arms a plan that ends underground without a word.
  It still flies it -- a deorbit is a manoeuvre -- but says so, and the
  burn page shows the new periapsis in red.

RE-ENTRY PLASMA, around the ship. The hull shader's windward tint was
invisible from any distance and absent in the cockpit. Exterior: a sheath,
a white-hot bow cap and a comet-tail wake with curling streamers. Cockpit:
the glass lights from where the air arrives and flame streams past it.
Both driven only by ship.reentryGlow and ship.windDir, held orange over
the tinted canopy so the ladder stays readable.

SHIELDS: blue when full, dull orange when empty, and the gradient between
is walked in OKLCh -- a straight mix of two near-opposites is grey at half
charge, measured on screen -- so it runs blue, violet, rose, orange. The
hit shimmers (two travelling ripples, scaled by charge, so a spent field
barely moves), lasts 0.9 s instead of 0.55, and the player's field is
fitted to the hull being flown rather than the courier.

THE DRIVE COMES OUT OF THE ENGINES. The plume was one point on the
centreline, sized for the procedural courier -- between the Talon's two
bells, so it lit their inboard sides -- and it swung to follow the thrust
vector, so a sideways or orbital-frame burn fired it out of a bell at
ninety degrees. Nozzles are read off the mesh now (Talon, Dart, Kestrel
two bells each at the measured x; Mule one wide stern), the main drive
only ever fires straight aft on the along-nose part of the thrust, and
the rest shows as RCS puffs from the hull.
